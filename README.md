# LabelGuard

### AI-Powered Legal Metrology Compliance Assistance Platform

LabelGuard is an AI-assisted compliance platform designed to help identify potential non-compliance in packaged commodities under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

It provides a multi-role compliance ecosystem connecting manufacturers, sellers, consumers, and Legal Metrology officers through a common workflow.

> **AI extracts. Rules validate. Humans decide.**

---

## SIH Problem Statement

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26034

**Title:** Software System to check compliance of Packaged Commodities under Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 by scanning products, images and labels.

### Problem

Packaged commodity compliance involves checking multiple mandatory declarations such as:

- Product/common or generic name
- Net quantity
- Maximum Retail Price (MRP)
- Manufacturer/Packer/Importer details
- Country of origin where applicable
- Manufacturing/Packing date
- Best Before / Use By where applicable
- Consumer care details
- Unit sale price where applicable

Manual verification can be time-consuming, especially when information is distributed across different package panels and online listings.
